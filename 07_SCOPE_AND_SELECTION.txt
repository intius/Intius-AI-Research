SCOPE AND SELECTION
Prepared 2026-09-25.

Method: whole-tree filename inventory, automated report discovery/content
screening, historical mechanism register, and targeted reading of selected
reports plus later corrections. 460 report/status/summary-like text files
under 150,000 bytes were discovered and loaded by the automated inventory.
This is not a claim of detailed human-style reading of every one of those
files or every raw example, source line, checkpoint and archive member.

Detailed source snapshots selected for the release are in 08_SOURCE_REGISTER.
Top-level research areas were screened through their reports or the existing
mechanism-history register. Coverage depth therefore varies by area.

Excluded from traversal: dependency runtimes, site-packages, node_modules,
.git, virtual environments and Python caches. Binary checkpoints and
archives were inventoried by filename but not exhaustively unpacked. In
particular !!!Current.rar was not used to assume additional distinct results.
Large aggregate TXT histories were navigation/history support, not dumped
into the release. Source scripts and raw benchmarks remain outside it.

Selection requires a measured positive signal, a useful specific detail,
enough source context to assess it, and an honest description of later
limitations. R cards target distinctive empirical observations. A cards
are explicitly optional established-method case studies useful to builders.
Nothing here certifies that an idea is publicly unknown.
