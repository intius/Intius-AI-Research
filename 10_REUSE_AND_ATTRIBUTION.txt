REUSE AND ATTRIBUTION

Suggested descriptive title:
Intius AI Research: Selected Local Research Findings, 2026-09-25.

The research notes were synthesized with AI assistance from the owner's
local experiment archive. Numerical observations come from the cited local
reports. They have not been peer reviewed or externally replicated by this
curation. Author identity and a final publication citation are for the owner
to supply; no author names or institutional affiliations were invented.

This release contains explanatory text, selected report snapshots and source
identifiers. It does not include pretrained weights, adapters, copied training
corpora, benchmark questions, private conversations or infrastructure logs.
Selected report metadata has been sanitized: local absolute paths, private
notebook links and private compute/account identifiers are replaced. Scientific
measurements and public scholarly references are retained. Source SHA-256s
identify the original bytes; packaged snapshots have different hashes.

No new license has been assigned on the owner's behalf. A public repository
intended for training reuse should state the owner's chosen terms for these
notes. Referenced third-party code, model weights and datasets retain their
own terms; this collection does not grant rights to those separate artifacts.
Public-source links in the LoRA recipe are attribution and provenance, not
redistribution of the datasets.

The selected findings are an editorial synthesis. Application suggestions
are explicitly prospective and do not add experiments to the evidence record.
