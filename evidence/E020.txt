0017 | The GRU's Wilson 95% interval is 90.85–93.02%. It beats the strongest
0018 | dendritic alternative by 18.46 percentage points; the paired episode-bootstrap
0019 | 95% interval is 16.83–20.13 points and McNemar `p=1.05e-117`.

0021 | Resetting the GRU before every event yields 270/2,400 (11.25%), establishing
0022 | that retained recurrent state supplies its result. The common frozen
0023 | backbone-plus-old-ten input scores 510/2,400 (21.25%) on this new wording.

0025 | ## What the controls establish
0026 | 
0027 | Removing elapsed-time decay improves the dendritic architecture from 46.00%
0028 | to 62.67%. Removing its branch value nonlinearity as well improves it to
0029 | 73.54%. The separately retrained controls therefore agree with the earlier
0030 | lesion direction, while showing that the earlier 98% inference-only lesion was
0031 | not a valid estimate of a retrained architecture.
0032 | 
0033 | The ordinary GRU is better, smaller in persistent state, faster to train, and
0034 | structurally simpler on this latest-method memory task. The brain-inspired
0035 | details do not earn their complexity here.
0036 | 
0037 | ## Existing benchmark
0038 | 
0039 | All three dendritic variants preserve TRIAD at 96/96 math, 96/96 grammar,
0040 | 48/48 grammar pairs, 96/96 coding cases and 6/6 coding tasks. The GRU records
0041 | 95/96 math and otherwise the same perfect scores. This is a one-item known-task
0042 | regression for the GRU.
0043 | 
0044 | The reused wording probe scores 11/15 for all dendritic variants and 12/15 for
0045 | the GRU. Every model still routes the queue description to `math_affine`, so
0046 | the queue result remains 0/5. None acquires a missing algorithm.

0048 | ## Corrected conclusion
0049 | 
0050 | The earlier 1,010-cell result demonstrated that increasing persistent capacity
0051 | can produce large memory behavior. It did not demonstrate an advantage over
0052 | ordinary recurrence. This fair parameter-matched test rejects that stronger
0053 | interpretation: **the regular GRU is decisively better for this task and uses
0054 | about thirteen times less persistent state.**
0055 | 
0056 | The current positive finding is narrower: a compact exact-tool library can gain
0057 | useful conversational method memory from a small recurrent controller. That is
0058 | an established hybrid design direction, not a new architecture or general
0059 | intelligence result. Confirmation remains sealed.
