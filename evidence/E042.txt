0001 | # Experiment BC — Protected BB passage reranker
0002 | 
0003 | Completed privately on Kaggle on 2026-09-19.
0004 | 
0005 | ## Result
0006 | 
0007 | BC passed every preregistered promotion gate. The primary pairwise XGBoost
0008 | ranker raised mean full-corpus token F1 from BB's 53.64% raw top-five selection
0009 | to 56.91%, a gain of 3.27 points. Every reader seed improved:
0010 | 
0011 | | BB seed | Raw top-5 F1 | Pairwise F1 | Gain |
0012 | |---|---:|---:|---:|
0013 | | 4101 | 53.02% | 56.26% | +3.24 |
0014 | | 4102 | 54.17% | 56.75% | +2.58 |
0015 | | 4103 | 53.73% | 57.73% | +4.01 |
0016 | 
0017 | The independent 3,201-parameter listwise MLP reached 56.94% mean F1, nearly
0018 | identical to the primary pairwise result. Mean top-five oracle F1 is 64.85%, so
0019 | 7.93 points of selection/reading headroom remain.
0020 | 
0021 | ## Integrity and efficiency
0022 | 
0023 | - All three BB checkpoint hashes were identical before and after inference.
0024 | - BB top-1, raw top-five and answerability predictions reproduced exactly.
0025 | - The complete 1,000-question/2,067-context validation corpus and frozen
0026 |   retrieval hash were preserved.
0027 | - All 14 manifest entries verified after the private archive was copied local.
0028 | - Reader feature extraction peaked at 207,316,992 allocated CUDA bytes in the
0029 |   largest branch; ranking ran on CPU.
0030 | - Host peak RSS was 3,036,246,016 bytes.
0031 | 
0032 | ## Decision
0033 | 
0034 | Promote `BB reader → BC pairwise ranker` as the strongest directly tested
0035 | non-LLM general-knowledge QA stack. The listwise MLP is retained as a confirming
0036 | secondary selector, but the preregistered pairwise model remains primary.
0037 | 
0038 | The next necessary test is zero-shot transfer beyond SQuAD. Experiment BD will
0039 | freeze BB and BC and evaluate them on deterministic answer-bearing HotpotQA
0040 | distractor questions without fitting any HotpotQA labels.
