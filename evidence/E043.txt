0001 | # Experiment BD — Zero-shot HotpotQA transfer
0002 | 
0003 | Completed privately on Kaggle on 2026-09-19.
0004 | 
0005 | ## Result
0006 | 
0007 | BD passed every preregistered transfer gate without fitting or selecting on a
0008 | HotpotQA label. Across the three frozen BB readers:
0009 | 
0010 | - Mean gold-context EM/F1: 34.60% / 51.76%.
0011 | - Mean retrieved top-1 EM/F1: 13.23% / 21.91%.
0012 | - Mean raw top-five EM/F1: 15.63% / 25.63%.
0013 | - Mean BC pairwise EM/F1: 16.20% / 27.22%.
0014 | - Mean BC listwise EM/F1: 16.60% / 27.29%.
0015 | - Mean top-five oracle EM/F1: 30.10% / 46.27%.
0016 | 
0017 | The primary pairwise selector improved raw selection by 1.60 F1 points in the
0018 | mean and by 1.15–2.03 points in every seed. This is genuine zero-shot transfer:
0019 | the rankers, feature normalization and reader weights came only from SQuAD.
0020 | 
0021 | ## Interpretation
0022 | 
0023 | The reader and selector both transfer, but absolute performance identifies the
0024 | next bottleneck. Character TF-IDF found an answer-bearing paragraph at rank 1
0025 | for 35.1% and within five for 72.6% of questions. Once the answer-bearing
0026 | paragraph was supplied, frozen BB reached 51.76% F1; within retrieved top five,
0027 | the oracle reached 46.27%, while pairwise selection reached only 27.22%.
0028 | Therefore cross-passage ranking—not basic extractive ability—is the largest
0029 | immediate recoverable gap on this domain.
0030 | 
0031 | Only 5.20% of fixed-vocabulary tokens were out of vocabulary. Reader feature
0032 | extraction peaked at 185,296,896 allocated CUDA bytes and the complete run took
0033 | 86 seconds.
0034 | 
0035 | ## Decision
0036 | 
0037 | Promote the claim that BB and BC have measurable cross-dataset transfer, while
0038 | retaining `BB → BC pairwise` as the SQuAD current-best stack. Experiment BE
0039 | must train a protected multi-domain ranker on HotpotQA training questions plus
0040 | the frozen SQuAD feature replay, then require a material HotpotQA gain without
0041 | forgetting BC's SQuAD performance.
