0042 | ## Fresh follow-up: what caused the library gain?
0043 | 
0044 | The first screen changed canonicalization and selection together. The follow-up
0045 | added raw marginal, uncapped raw marginal, and canonical MAP controls. It used
0046 | 3,072 fresh worlds and retained the original training data and frozen neural
0047 | reference. Seven methods were evaluated; this is not seven new training seeds.
0048 | The follow-up took 25.80 seconds in the recorded VM process.
0049 | 
0050 | | Method | Fresh primary accuracy | Stored numeric state |
0051 | | --- | ---: | ---: |
0052 | | Raw library, MAP | 58.64% | 77,824 bytes |
0053 | | Raw library, marginal | 58.69% | 77,824 bytes |
0054 | | Uncapped raw library, marginal | 61.72% | 129,523 bytes |
0055 | | Canonical library, MAP | 62.55% | 21,280 bytes |
0056 | | Canonical library, marginal | 63.33% | 21,280 bytes |
0057 | | Canonical library, persistent | 63.33% | 21,280 bytes |
0058 | | Frozen relation-conditioned neural mixture | 23.93% | 18,080 bytes |
0059 | 
0060 | The canonical marginal library gains 4.64 percentage points over the capped raw
0061 | marginal control while retaining approximately 3.66x less numeric state. The
0062 | paired, multiplicity-adjusted p-value is approximately 9.83e-10 for these sampled
0063 | synthetic worlds. Against the uncapped raw library, its point score is 1.61
0064 | points higher with approximately 6.09x less state, but the adjusted p-value is
0065 | 0.0782; accuracy superiority over that stronger control is uncertain.
0066 | 
0067 | Persistent hypotheses versus canonical marginalization: 40 gains and 40 losses
0068 | on the fresh primary cases, exactly zero aggregate gain. The special persistent
0069 | identity hypothesis therefore has no confirmed overall benefit here. Its unit
0070 | test is mathematically correct, but that does not establish practical superiority.
0071 | 
0072 | Canonicalization here subtracts an operator's output at state zero and stores
0073 | the shared shape; an observed input/output pair determines its output offset.
0074 | That exploits an explicit modular output-translation symmetry. Canonicalization,
0075 | symmetry reuse and Bayesian inference have established precedents. This result
0076 | does not certify a novel AI principle.

0081 | ## Important failure: genuinely different rules
0082 | 
0083 | The canonical marginal library scores 91.80% on ordinary new worlds and 82.62%
0084 | on long compositions, but only 18.36% on the new cubic-rule family. The compact
0085 | neural mixture scores 29.30% on that new family, yet only 23.93% overall in the
0086 | fresh follow-up. Neither is a generally capable reasoner.
0087 | 
0088 | Full-evidence and renamed-state diagnostics are solved exactly by many methods
0089 | because their supplied composition algorithm can follow an explicit complete
0090 | table. These easy-to-certify execution results are deliberately excluded from
0091 | the primary score and cannot be used as an intelligence claim.

0093 | ## Novelty and scope
0094 | 
0095 | Ten initial configurations tested experimental combinations around six broad
0096 | mechanisms: evidence anchoring, residual witness recurrence, adaptive temperature,
0097 | relative transport, adaptive permutation constraints, and persistent hypotheses.
0098 | Their controls and prior art are in [NOVELTY.md](NOVELTY.md). They are not ten
0099 | verified inventions, and this campaign is not a breakthrough or a 1B/100B result.
