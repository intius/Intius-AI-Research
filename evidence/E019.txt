0044 | 
0045 | - The prerequisite signal-normalization experiment was complete and its entire manifest verified before these runs.
0046 | - Direct per-pool mean aggregation in fixed units, frozen standardization, then unit-RMS actual decoder input. Same rule at all N/modes; no legacy sum is fed to the decoder, no per-size gain is fitted. Every event RMS was checked.
0047 | - Same exact examples, order, cached hidden80 features, backbone logits, labels, elapsed-time inputs, tools, knowledge, update equation and code.
0048 | - Same canonical cell-configuration prefix at every N; graph seeds change only random recurrent endpoints. Ring-plus-two-random-neighbors algorithm at all sizes, including 50k. Five genuinely different graphs at 50k as well.
0049 | - Shuffling permutes signed outgoing messages anew each sweep, without changing stored graph or cross-mixing episode state. B1 and B2 share the exact same seeded permutation stream.
0050 | - No recurrence retains local memory; erased-state zeros all six fields before every event and retains two within-event sweeps.
0051 | - Every rule/decoder value is bit-identical within its branch. B1 decoder equals A exactly; B2 decoder equals its completed jointly trained checkpoint. No neuron/edge-indexed trainable tensor exists.

0081 | ## Interpretation boundary
0082 | 
0083 | A size-dependent recall change under these controls is a result about frozen-checkpoint inference on this specific test. It is not proof that independent memory capacity, useful recurrent reasoning, or general intelligence caused the difference. Extra fixed features, pool coverage, averaging, graph geometry and closeness to the original 50k training distribution still change when cells are added.
0084 | In particular, judge useful inter-cell recurrence by normal-versus-no-recurrence and normal-versus-shuffled comparisons, not merely by a large-versus-small score. Judge persistence separately with erased-state results. Prefix selection is fixed here; these are graph seeds, not multiple training seeds or independently sampled cell-feature banks.
0085 | The unit-RMS operation is the same previously tested control and is fixed everywhere; it also changes the original trained decoder-input distribution globally. These results must not be substituted for the earlier unnormalized 50k scores.
