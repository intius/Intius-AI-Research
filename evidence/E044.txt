0001 | # Experiment BE — Protected multi-domain passage ranking
0002 | 
0003 | Completed privately on Kaggle on 2026-09-19.
0004 | 
0005 | ## Result
0006 | 
0007 | BE preserved SQuAD but failed the preregistered HotpotQA capability gate. The
0008 | mixed pairwise primary reached 28.05% mean HotpotQA F1 versus BD's 27.22%, only
0009 | +0.83 point rather than the required +3.00. Per-seed gains were +0.52, +1.29
0010 | and +0.68 points, so neither Hotpot promotion check passed.
0011 | 
0012 | The confirming mixed listwise MLP performed better at 28.48% F1, and the
0013 | Hotpot-only pairwise control reached 28.10%. Both remain far below the frozen
0014 | 46.27% top-five oracle.
0015 | 
0016 | ## Preservation and integrity
0017 | 
0018 | - Mean SQuAD F1 changed by only -0.14 point; every seed stayed within the
0019 |   predeclared 0.5-point retention boundary.
0020 | - All BB reader hashes and all BC feature hashes remained unchanged.
0021 | - Exact 8,000-question Hotpot training, 8,000-question SQuAD replay and
0022 |   1,000-question validation sizes verified.
0023 | - All 17 manifest entries verified after copying the private outputs locally.
0024 | - Peak CUDA allocation was 186,746,880 bytes; ranking ran on CPU.
0025 | 
0026 | ## Interpretation
0027 | 
0028 | The result rules out insufficient target examples or simple source forgetting
0029 | as the main explanation. Even 8,000 labeled Hotpot groups plus protected SQuAD
0030 | replay moved pairwise ranking less than one F1 point. The current 16 scalar
0031 | features do not encode enough cross-passage semantics to approach the oracle.
0032 | The next credible experiment should add richer non-LLM passage-question and
0033 | answer-context interaction features while keeping the BB reader protected,
0034 | rather than increasing ranker data or tree capacity again.
0035 | 
0036 | ## Decision
0037 | 
0038 | Do not promote BE. Retain BC as the SQuAD ranker and BD as the verified
0039 | zero-shot transfer result. Keep BE's listwise checkpoint as a diagnostic only.
