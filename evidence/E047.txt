0006 | EXECUTIVE RESULT
0007 | The pinned local MiniCPM5-1B-Q4 model produced no complete structured writer
0008 | records under the frozen four-statement/96-token write budget. All48 writes hit
0009 | the output cap and failed the required `events` JSON schema; the PCBL and
0010 | same-writer structured controls therefore received zero of192 gold facts and
0011 | zero of52 corrections. Their equal PCBL/full-recompute states are both empty
0012 | and are not evidence of a working memory system.
0013 | 
0014 | The reader did not pass the supplied-memory diagnostic either. The oracle PCBL
0015 | with gold facts answered the current city alone on8/12 cases, but gave exact
0016 | city+source on only3/12 current questions and0/12 historical questions. No
0017 | arm produced a fully correct current-and-historical pair. The prompt used
0018 | `[source: T##]` literally; the model repeated that placeholder in160/168
0019 | answers. Citation and provenance scores are invalid as a measure of whether it
0020 | could cite evidence. The protocol also capped each answer at32 output tokens.
0021 | 
0022 | Decision: this is an INCONCLUSIVE end-to-end language-memory study with a
0023 | clear writer-budget/format bottleneck and a reader-prompt confound. It is not a
0024 | negative result for all revisable memory, not a clean evaluation of PCBL, and
0025 | not a VRAM/capability win. The exact measured pipeline is retired; do not rerun
0026 | it unchanged.

0121 | INFERRED — WHAT FAILED
0122 | The batch writer did not fit the expected structured output into96 tokens. It
0123 | also did not follow the requested `events` schema or relation canonicalization.
0124 | The specific cause is not isolated: output truncation, JSON instruction
0125 | following, model capacity, template quality and relation/entity extraction all
0126 | remain possible. Since every writer call hit the cap, the test cannot establish
0127 | whether MiniCPM5 would parse the same facts with enough output space or an
0128 | atomic constrained writer.
0129 | 
0130 | The reader's source placeholder was a protocol defect: it wrote `T##` as
0131 | literal example text, and160/168 answers repeated it. The next reader protocol
0132 | must request an actual turn identifier, explicitly say not to output the
0133 | placeholder, and include a concrete non-target example or a structured
0134 | response check before scoring citations. The oracle historical failure means
0135 | the reader still needs its own fresh development test after writer success.
0136 | 
0137 | The identical empty learned PCBL/full state is a degenerate parity check. The
0138 | zero update operation counts and approximately equal empty-store bytes do not
0139 | show an efficiency gain. The raw text controls' city-only scores show limited
0140 | partial recall on this generated corpus but no complete factual answers.

0168 | PROPOSED — NEXT DISTINCT QUESTION
0169 | Do atomic transaction writes with constrained JSON decoding let the pinned
0170 | local model acquire exact facts and corrections under a realistic per-event
0171 | token budget? Use a new generated seed and new entity strings, leaving PROMO-1
0172 | sealed. Compare two matched writer arms on every single event: (1) ordinary
0173 | one-event JSON prompting and (2) llama.cpp JSON-schema constrained decoding.
0174 | Only one assertion is generated per call; both arms use the same input, model,
0175 | greedy policy, maximum completion tokens and per-event source-turn budget.
0176 | The earlier four-event free-form V3 writer is a historical control, not rerun.
0177 | 
0178 | Score JSON validity, exact triple precision/recall, relation canonicalization,
0179 | source-turn accuracy, correction operation/supersedes accuracy, valid
0180 | assertions per input/completion token and CPU seconds. Feed both parsed streams
0181 | through PCBL and full recomputation offline; require exact state parity on
0182 | current/historical facts. Advancement gate: ≥90% exact fact rows and ≥90% exact
0183 | correction/source rows on fresh development entities; below that, stop
0184 | language-memory integration and do not run reader or PROMO-1 promotion.
