0009 | DECISION

0011 | test. It does NOT pass the broader assistant requirement. Retain the mathematical
0012 | component for research; do not call this a frontier replacement or assume huge
0013 | potential from this result. A domain-specific text renderer does not supply
0014 | general language understanding. No claim of global novelty is established.

0016 | WHAT IS NEW RELATIVE TO OUR RETIRED LIBRARY PATH
0017 | The model infers coordinate functions from action-induced differences, then
0018 | learns sparse conditional rules and an observation decoder. It is not choosing
0019 | among the old 48 transformations or a stored solution-fragment library. It
0020 | learns the coefficients of its representation and dynamics from observations.
0021 | Finite-field algebra, system identification and invariant learning are known
0022 | areas; originality of the precise estimator has not been established.
0023 | 
0024 | FORMULATION
0025 | For observation y, learn q_i(y) as a low-degree Boolean polynomial. For each
0026 | observed action a, differences constrain all coordinates simultaneously:
0027 |   q_i(y_next) XOR q_i(y) = 1 only if a=i and a change occurred.
0028 | The solver learns a decoder back to observations and sparse Boolean gates in
0029 | the inferred coordinates. Ordinary regression separately fits next-state
0030 | differences in raw observations. No true latent variables enter ordinary fitting.
0031 | 
0032 | STRONG PRIOR AND SCOPE
0033 | Each action changes at most one independent Boolean coordinate. Gates have at
0034 | most three parents; inverse observation coordinates are degree one or two.
0035 | The benchmark uses opaque 8/12-bit observations under identity, invertible
0036 | linear, or quadratic encodings. These are deliberately structured synthetic
0037 | systems, not a representative general-intelligence benchmark. One model is
0038 | trained per world/dimension; larger dimensions are not zero-shot size transfer.
