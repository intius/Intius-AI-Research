0001 | # Neural ABI stage 2 — learned standard and modular transfer
0002 | 
0003 | 
0004 | **Read with the [frozen stress results](STRESS_REPORT.md).** The controlled gates
0005 | pass, but reordered descriptions reduce ABI accuracy to 25–38%, and 128-step chains
0006 | retain only about 49% whole-state correctness. The main scores do not establish
0007 | general text understanding or long-chain reliability.
0008 | 
0009 | This follow-up replaces the supplied four-number linear protocol with a learned
0010 | 24-dimensional code, uses controlled-text MLP/GRU/Transformer clients, attaches
0011 | a new capability after encoders freeze, and tests repeated port-to-port transfers.
0012 | All results below are actual categorical accuracies, not the previous regression
0013 | score. They are not directly comparable to stage 1's 0.9985 score.
0014 | 
0015 | The standard is learned by a reference autoencoder, then frozen and shared as
0016 | a training target. Clients have independent ordinary weights and disjoint fitting
0017 | states. The experimenter still supplies the categorical world, word meanings,
0018 | training labels, loss and standard-defining procedure. This is reference-based
0019 | interoperability, not spontaneous agreement between independently learned semantics.

0040 | The posthoc adapter is a separate nonlinear 24->128->24 model per encoder,
0041 | trained after freezing on 1,024 calibration states for 800 updates. It adds 25,184
0042 | parameter bytes per deployed encoder. ABI clients receive public-code supervision
0043 | throughout their 8,192-state fitting set and additionally train heads on clean code.

0045 | a claim that ABI intrinsically beats every equally resourced alignment method.
0046 | Only three independent seed groups were trained. Directed swaps share models and
0047 | are not independent replications.

0064 | ## Multi-step communication

0068 | predicted codes feed subsequent modules, with no ground-truth repair between steps.

0100 | 
0101 | The exact 12-byte categorical-state protocol and supplied operations solve these
0102 | tests 100% by construction. The learned 24-float neural packet uses 96 bytes before
0103 | any model overhead, eight times more than the byte-per-field representation.
0104 | Because four values fit in two bits, the theoretical packed state is only 3 bytes;
0105 | the 12-byte control is deliberately a simple uncompressed format. Neither is a
0106 | general language model, but they prevent treating this experiment as evidence
0107 | that neural messages are inherently the smallest useful communication format.

0135 | Native runtime probes perform one state readout. ABI probes include a text client,
0136 | its head, three noise-trained operator networks and four supplied operation calls.
0137 | They do more work, so the latency ratio is not an efficiency comparison on the
0138 | same capability. Memory probes use each client's own head; scored chains use the
0139 | MLP lab head, which has identical tensor shapes. The teacher is not loaded in
0140 | either deployment. Probe results use seed 711, batch 1; MB means 1,000,000 bytes.
0141 | Full-process high-water marks include Python/PyTorch and checkpoint loading.
0142 | CUDA tensor allocated/reserved counters are preserved separately in runtime JSONs.

0144 | The frozen reference has 75,040 parameter bytes plus 192 normalization-buffer bytes.
0145 | Vocabulary/tokenization code, reference checkpoints, schemas and all learned module
0146 | weights are separately archived. Each late head adds 87,104 parameter bytes if
0147 | retained. Each operator adds 91,232 parameter bytes. A deployed collection's cost
0148 | is the sum of its loaded components and working state, not merely a 96-byte port.

0156 | ## Claim boundary and evidence
0157 | 
0158 | This is a controlled compositional scene domain with a fixed vocabulary and a
0159 | shared learned reference. It does not establish natural conversation, advanced
0160 | reasoning, a universal neural standard, automatic creation of new representational
0161 | concepts, growing protocol vocabulary or a general performance-per-RAM advantage.
0162 | No source weights from an existing LLM were quantized or compressed.
0163 | 
0164 | The substantive test is whether the original interface result survives a learned
0165 | code, different client architectures, a later head and repeated neural transfers.
0166 | Keep the successful and failed gates distinct. These known building blocks are
0167 | not claimed unprecedented; the earlier literature review includes
0168 | [zero-shot latent communication](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.15430).
0169 | 
0170 | PROTOCOL.md was frozen before launch. ANALYSIS_NOTES.md clarified deployment-only
0171 | chain reporting before any training results were inspected. AUDIT.json verifies
0172 | data separation including intermediate paths, independently reconstructs labels
0173 | and recomputes every metric. REPLAY.json records a frozen CPU replay. Full source,
0174 | states, predictions, fitting curves and checkpoints are in the verified archive.
