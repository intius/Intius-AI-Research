0001 | # Stage 2: broad, checked post-training — frozen 2026-09-24
0002 | 
0003 | This is an **established supervised distillation/replay recipe**, not a claimed new architecture. The first LoRA adapter remains the fallback. The user authorized stronger post-training and later exploration of genuinely different methods. No paid compute, publication, hosting or public model upload is involved.
0004 | 
0005 | ## Why this stage
0006 | 
0007 | The first adapter finishes short synthetic reasoning tasks much more often than its base, but its 8/24 result on a small public MMLU-Pro/GSM8K subset is inconclusive. SmolLM3's relaxed arithmetic score is higher under the same cap. We will test whether stronger math examples, coding and instruction-following data can add breadth without erasing the first adapter's short-answer behavior.
