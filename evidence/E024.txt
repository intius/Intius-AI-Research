0001 | # Experiment BM — Learned matching versus an identity-matching prior
0002 | 
0003 | ## Decision
0004 | 
0005 | **Primary arm A passed every preregistered one-hop gate. Retain it only as a
0006 | learned one-hop binding component. Do not launch multi-hop training and do not
0007 | open BK confirmation.**
0008 | 
0009 | All three arms reached 100% final endpoint accuracy and 100% both-members
0010 | correctness on all four diagnostic views. Their scientific meanings differ:
0011 | 
0012 | - **A independently learned matching.** Its correct-row attention began near
0013 |   chance (0.83–1.03%) and reached 100% on every view using final destination
0014 |   labels only.
0015 | - **B began with an architectural matching prior.** Its shared normalized
0016 |   query/key map selected the correct row 100% before training. Training sharpened
0017 |   attention, but did not establish discovery of the matching rule.
0018 | - **C kept that matching geometry frozen.** Its geometry hash never changed,
0019 |   while endpoint accuracy rose from chance to 100%. This isolates learned
0020 |   temperature/content/answer decoding under an engineered row-selection prior.
0021 | 
0022 | This is a strong memory-access component result, not novel reasoning, a joint
0023 | reasoner, or evidence of general intelligence.

0106 | ## Attribution
0107 | 
0108 | Arm A is the key positive result: it started without correct-row access and
0109 | learned a reliable source-operation binding from final endpoint loss. Because
0110 | its attention and endpoints both transfer perfectly to unbiased sampling,
0111 | destination reassignment and consistent entity renaming, this is evidence for
0112 | learned neural matching plus learned content decoding.
0113 | 
0114 | B and C do not independently establish learned matching. Both select the
0115 | correct row before training because identical normalized mappings are applied
0116 | to identical source-operation tuples. C proves that, once this prior supplies
0117 | the row, the common module can learn to decode arbitrary destination content.
0118 | B sharpens attention geometry, but B and C have a zero-point endpoint gap on
0119 | every view. Under the frozen rule this establishes no material endpoint benefit
0120 | from training B's shared geometry in this screen.
0121 | 
0122 | BL remains the historical control at 1.8555% endpoint accuracy. BM changes the
0123 | complete addressing/content module, so BM versus BL does not isolate one layer,
0124 | normalization choice, or contamination effect.
0125 | 
0126 | ## Claim boundary
0127 | 
0128 | BM demonstrates one-hop associative memory access. It does not test multi-hop
0129 | state reuse, procedure learning, arithmetic integration, broad knowledge,
0130 | continual learning, or general reasoning. Successful one-hop lookup is not
0131 | novel reasoning. The result is returned to the orchestrator without selecting
0132 | or launching a subsequent experiment.
