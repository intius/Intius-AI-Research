0001 | # Idea 004 — Neural ABI: controlled compatibility result
0002 | 
0003 | The original coordinate-penalty implementation failed its compatibility gate.
0004 | One explicitly declared follow-up additionally trained decoders on exact protocol
0005 | messages. It **passed** the compatibility gate. Both results are preserved.
0006 | Scores below are 1 minus mean variance-normalized squared error across eight numerical outputs. They are not percentages of questions answered correctly, intelligence scores, or measures of general capability.

0016 | ## Interpretation
0017 | 
0018 | This tests independently trained encoder/head combinations without a learned
0019 | pair-specific adapter, using a deliberately supplied semantic protocol. Each lab
0020 | has its own sensor rotation, fitting data and ordinary model weights. The shared
0021 | code encodes four state coordinates; it does not supply the eight answer outputs.
0022 | The three architecture designs are dense, residual and gated MLPs. This is not a
0023 | demonstration across language, vision and robotics foundation models.
0024 | 
0025 | The original heads' mean score on exact public codes is
0026 | 0.869646, compared with
0027 | 0.995853 on their own ports and
0028 | 0.870689 after removing
0029 | the nominally unused 12-dimensional subspace. These diagnostics assess dependence
0030 | on private deviations; they do not prove which individual coordinates encode
0031 | which learned computations. The corrected training gives each decoder examples
0032 | of the exact agreed code, closing that unsupervised part of the contract.
0033 | 
0034 | The follow-up's expanded-domain cross score is
0035 | 0.969575. This diagnostic samples [-1.3,1.3]
0036 | and includes some in-domain points. No language/OOD universality claim follows.
0037 | 
0038 | ## Replication units and cost
0039 | 
0040 | Three independent training seeds; six directed swaps per seed share trained
0041 | components and are not 18 independent training replications. Cross scores by seed:
0042 | 
0043 | - Seed 401: original 0.841581; conformance 0.998244.
0044 | - Seed 402: original 0.897439; conformance 0.998637.
0045 | - Seed 403: original 0.848656; conformance 0.998625.
0046 | 
0047 | Original: 18 fits, 1600 updates each; total reported fitting time
0048 | 68.56s. Extension: 9 fits, same update count,
0049 | 42.29s. The extension adds a head-only
0050 | forward/backward on exact protocol messages; it is not compute-identical to the
0051 | original. All models start randomly; no pretrained LLM or quantization is used.
0052 | The public code uses 256 bytes in float32, the sensor rotation 64 bytes per lab,
0053 | and each port message 64 bytes. An affine adapter is 17 x 16 float32 values = 1,088 bytes,
0054 | plus file/runtime overhead, and needs 256 calibration states per pair.

0065 | The ABI pair memory probe assembles a dense encoder and residual decoder from
0066 | the original standard arm. The corrected arm has the same tensor shapes but was
0067 | not separately measured. Inference discards unused parts of both source models.
0068 | ABI does not inherently reduce the parameter size of this composed model.

0070 | ## Limits and prior art
0071 | 
0072 | This is engineered numerical interoperability. No conversational ability,
0073 | advanced mathematics, learned universal semantics, general-model RAM advantage
0074 | or ability to connect arbitrary third-party models was demonstrated. The shared
0075 | semantic code is supplied by the experimenter. The correction was selected after
0076 | the first result, and its positive evidence needs independent follow-up.
0077 | Zero-shot stitching already exists in
0078 | [Relative Representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.15430); this pilot does not
0079 | establish worldwide novelty.
