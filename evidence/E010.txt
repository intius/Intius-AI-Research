0001 | # ABI repairs versus original small LLMs
0002 | 
0003 | **Read the [plain-word diagnostic](SCALAR_REPORT.md) with this report.** The
0004 | original output contract was a poor basis for a reasoning comparison: the0.5B
0005 | model often returned the wrong list length, while the1.5B run was intentionally
0006 | interrupted after repeated truncation. Unrun cases are not counted as failures.
0007 | The simpler comparison was completed for both models, but is explicitly posthoc.
0008 | 
0009 | This study compares a permutation-invariant set encoder, an ordinary GRU with
0010 | order augmentation, the frozen original ABI, and unmodified Qwen2.5-0.5B/1.5B
0011 | instruction models. Six new neural fits completed. LLMs use full FP16 weights,
0012 | with no extra quantization or parameter removal.
0013 | 
0014 | The 192 test cases cover 32 new scenes in two layouts and three tasks. ABI models
0015 | have been specifically trained on this scene schema and receive task routing
0016 | through a structured API. LLMs receive a definition sheet and three examples.
0017 | These are different training advantages and output interfaces. The comparison
0018 | measures this specialist workload; it cannot rank general intelligence.
0019 |

0078 | LLM memory and latency rows above come from the completed plain-word runs; ABI
0079 | runtime rows come from producing full vectors on the primary cases. The same
0080 | complete model components are resident, but the prompts and output workloads
0081 | differ. Treat these as resource observations, not a rigorously matched speedup.
0082 | 
0083 | MB means 1,000,000 bytes. LLM latency includes prefill and generation after
0084 | tokenization. ABI latency includes constructing the token tensor and obtaining
0085 | the fixed-size integer output. Neither measures an entire application UI. Their
0086 | output mechanisms differ; a fixed classifier does less work than generating prose.
0087 | VRAM ratios here apply only to this workload, not equal general capability.
0088 | 
0089 | The set network's invariance is supplied by known Deep Sets structure. It is
0090 | not a newly discovered reasoning algorithm. All inputs still use four objects,
0091 | three known fields and a finite vocabulary. Ordinary order augmentation is the
0092 | relevant repair control, not merely the original failing GRU. A handwritten
0093 | executor of the supplied rules solves these cases by construction; that is a
0094 | designed specialist, not learned general intelligence.
0095 | 
0096 | ## General-language coverage
0097 | 
0098 | ABI has no free-text generation head and cannot represent the 12 general requests
0099 | through this scene API. Its supported coverage is 0/12, separately from accuracy;
0100 | no fabricated ABI prose is scored. Qwen outputs for all 12 requests are preserved
0101 | in GENERAL_OUTPUTS.md and reviewed against the frozen rubrics in GENERAL_REVIEW.md.
0102 | That small manual screen is not a public benchmark or a scalar intelligence score.
0103 | 
0104 | There is no legitimate single general-performance-per-MB ratio here. A conditional
0105 | specialist advantage cannot be sold as retaining an LLM's broad abilities in a
0106 | small model. This experiment does not achieve the user's general-model objective.
