0001 | TRAINING EFFICIENCY: RESEARCH ASSESSMENT
0002 | 2026-09-07. Work performed solo and locally.
0003 | 
0004 | The fresh, controlled comparison reached matched test quality with 40.7% less timed wall time and 40.4% less process CPU time.
0005 | This is a local implementation and measurement of progressive-training ideas.
0006 | It does not establish a new universal scientific breakthrough or a GPU-scale speedup.
0007 | 
0008 | THE USEFUL RECIPE
0009 | Start with a shallow active network, use its cheaper updates to process more
0010 | training examples, then grow to the desired full architecture. Pace the
0011 | learning rate and growth by estimated computation rather than optimizer-step
0012 | count. Copy trained neighboring features into added layers, zero their output
0013 | projections at insertion, retain the old optimizer states, and let the new
0014 | layers learn. Stop after the fully expanded model meets the validation target.
0015 | 
0016 | The tested conservative schedule uses 4, then 8, then 12 blocks. Growth occurs
0017 | after one quarter and one half of the computation budget. All final models
0018 | have the same standard 12-block architecture; no teacher or extra inference
0019 | branch is required. New parameters are independent after copying.
0020 | 
0021 | An aggressive 1-to-12 variant was also tested. It used many more cheap early
0022 | updates and obtained especially good final sampled loss on the larger corpus,
0023 | but was less consistent on the smaller corpus. It is not a universal default.
0024 |

0030 | active. The progressive trainer actually stops and saves that early model.

0056 | EARLIER EVIDENCE AND NEGATIVE RESULTS
0057 | 1. Gradient prediction: raw unbiasedness checks passed, but the tested cheap
0058 |    head-gradient anchor predictors had poor prospective gradient alignment.
0059 |    I rejected that implementation before relying on skipped backward passes.
0060 | 2. Fixed-update growth: the moment-inheritance candidate ran faster per fixed
0061 |    training schedule but lost too much quality; its original gate failed.
0062 | 3. Computation-paced growth: allowing cheap phases to see more examples was
0063 |    substantially more useful than merely reducing work per iteration.

0094 | REFERENCE-RULE CORRECTION
0095 | The original three-seed WikiText timing gate required two passing readings
0096 | even from the model defining the target. One baseline reached its own final
0097 | band only on the last reading, leaving that reference time undefined.
0098 | Those original gates remain: growth=fail, cancellation=fail.
0099 | I did not widen the loss tolerance or relabel those gates. A separate rule
0100 | was written before seed 5801: earliest single passing baseline versus two
0101 | passing candidate readings, with full-test verification. CPU-time recording
0102 | and fixed process affinity were added because earlier wall times varied.
0103 | This chronology and the one-seed scope of the corrected check are important.
0104 | 
0105 | THE MORE NOVEL CANDIDATE DID NOT EARN ITS COMPLEXITY
0106 | Temporary cancellation inserts x + R_theta(x) - a*R_initial(x), starting
0107 | with a=1 and fading it to zero. At insertion it preserves outputs and old
0108 | raw gradients while permitting nonzero internal gradients in the new layer.
0109 | The frozen anchor still differentiates through its input; it is discarded
0110 | after the fade, leaving the standard inference model. Algebra and numerical
0111 | checks support those properties in the deterministic implementation.
0112 | They do not prove better convergence. Global clipping and adaptive optimizer
0113 | state can also change effective updates despite raw-gradient preservation.
0114 | The tested cancellation did not consistently beat simpler growth, so it is
0115 | not the recommended result or a validated new breakthrough. Cancellation/
0116 | centering and progressive growth also have substantial prior art.
0117 | 
0118 | SCOPE AND LIMITS
0119 | - CPU experiments on small byte-level language models with 64-byte contexts.
0120 | - Real trained models at 550,464 and 1,212,768 parameters; no 1B model was trained.
0121 | - No GPU, distributed-training, monetary-cost or energy reduction was measured.
0122 | - Memory-resident text and a small 256-symbol output head favor cheap early phases.
0123 | - This does not establish improvement in general reasoning/chat capability.
0124 | - AdamW and the stated reference settings were tested, not every optimized baseline.
0125 | - The final peak model capacity remains unchanged; peak training memory savings
0126 |   are not claimed. Snapshot/anchor storage is counted separately where used.
0127 | - No architecture or optimization principle has been established as novel by
0128 |   an exhaustive prior-art review. This is an internally checked local result.
