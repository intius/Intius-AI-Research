0001 | # Experiment AC — Width and training-horizon scaling
0002 | 
0003 | ## Verdict
0004 | 
0005 | **PASS. Promote a scalable addressed finite-register execution component.**
0006 | 
0007 | The preregistered width-512/8K model reached 98.42% mean frozen-Y transfer, 99.78% exhaustive local-transition accuracy, and 97.01% on depth-32/eight-register extrapolation. Every promotion gate passed.
0008 | 
0009 | A smaller 75K/8K model also reached 96.96% frozen transfer and 93.38% extended accuracy, making it the efficient candidate. The result shows that Y's apparent 50% architecture ceiling was an operation-capacity/training threshold, not a failure of learned addressing or recurrent composition.
0010 | 
0011 | This remains a synthetic finite-state execution result. Operations, value domain and instruction schema are fixed; it is not evidence of language understanding, open-ended reasoning, or AGI.

0079 | ## Decision and next boundary
0080 | 
0081 | Promote the shared smooth transition plus learned content addressing as a reliable scalable executor within the tested finite-state instruction family. Use 75K/8K when efficiency matters and 114K/8K as the quality reference.
0082 | 
0083 | The next experiment must change semantics rather than merely add depth: hold out entire operations, change the value domain, and require learning a new operation from demonstrations with frozen slow weights. That will test whether this is a reusable cognitive substrate or only a well-trained interpreter for a fixed algebra.
