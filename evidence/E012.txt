0001 | # Completed second compact-reasoning campaign
0002 | 
0003 | **31 configurations this round:** twenty neural prototypes and ten non-LLM methods in the screen, followed by one exact parameter-matched neural ablation.
0004 | Six screen controls repeat earlier definitions; initialization repeats and repeated evaluations are not additional configurations.

0044 | ## Did interference itself help?
0045 | 
0046 | On the third fresh set, three initializations of the coherent model averaged **33.54%**, versus **27.47%** for the exact one-coordinate incoherent control: **+6.07 points**.
0047 | 
0048 | Both have **4,520 trainable real values / 18,080 bytes** and start each paired run from the same weights. The ablation changes summing squared responses to squaring summed responses, introducing evidence cross terms. It retains the context mixer, supplied execution, anchoring, examples, batch order, update budget and selection procedure.

0066 | | symbol_library_evidence_mixture | 35.55% | 58.20% | 19.53% | 20.31% | 46.48% |
0067 | | symbol_library_persistent | 36.72% | 58.98% | 19.92% | 20.31% | 46.09% |
0068 | | born_real_interference | 33.20% | 37.50% | 20.70% | 26.17% | 32.81% |
0069 | 
0070 | Five examples determine a degree-at-most-four polynomial over the supplied finite field. High cubic/quartic performance from interpolation is therefore credited to a handwritten mathematical prior. Cubic rules were absent from training but development-visible in this round. They are not an untouched out-of-distribution test.
0071 | 
0072 | Most structured methods solve complete-evidence execution by the supplied interpreter. Those two diagnostic slices are excluded from primary accuracy. Direct sequence models must learn more of the execution algorithm and stayed weak under this budget; they are synthetic-token architecture probes, not general language models.
0073 | 
0074 | Equal updates do not establish equal compute or convergence. Neural training used one seed per architecture in the screen, with selected repeats only. Poor results do not establish that an architecture cannot work at larger scale or with different training.
