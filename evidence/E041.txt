0001 | # Experiment BB — Static-knowledge character BiDAF reader
0002 | 
0003 | ## Verdict
0004 | 
0005 | **PASS. Promote BB as the strongest non-LLM reader.**
0006 | 
0007 | Static GloVe vectors, character morphology and deeper BiDAF-style attention
0008 | raise mean gold-context F1 to 67.72% and full-corpus raw top-five F1 to 53.64%.
0009 | BB beats the promoted AZ+BA system by 7.50 F1 points despite using no
0010 | contextual language-model weights.
0011 | 
0012 | Every hard-negative branch passes independently. The result is not a favorable
0013 | seed or a transient single-run improvement.

0033 | ## Why it improved
0034 | 
0035 | - GloVe supplies static distributional knowledge without contextual or
0036 |   generative language-model weights; 92.28% of the 50,000-word vocabulary has
0037 |   a pretrained vector.
0038 | - Character CNNs add morphology and spelling information.
0039 | - Trilinear BiDAF attention represents detailed question/context interactions
0040 |   rather than only a mean question vector.
0041 | - Two recurrent modeling layers refine answer boundaries after cross-attention.
0042 | 
0043 | The answerability head again underperforms raw span scores for cross-passage
0044 | selection. A protected BB-specific pairwise ranker remains justified.
