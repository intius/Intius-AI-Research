0017 | ROUND 1: EXECUTABLE RULE INDUCTION WITH CONTINUATION MARGINALS
0018 | The engine selects consistent executable rules from a 289-function prior,
0019 | composes the surviving assignments, and probes to reduce uncertainty about the
0020 | requested answer. It retains evidence across changing programs. A compiled
0021 | answer-conditioned marginal replaces repeated scans of the joint hypotheses.
0022 | The score itself is ordinary answer-aware active learning; its implementation
0023 | was the candidate contribution. There is no neural model or token objective.
0024 | 
0025 | Confirmation: 3 fresh seeds, 24 worlds, 192 tasks/method, 4 short then 4 long
0026 | programs/world. Long programs contain 16, 24, 32 or 48 unknown-operation call
0027 | instructions, sometimes conditional. All methods get at most 24 oracle replies
0028 | per task. A direct executor can consequently abstain on a longer program.

0085 | ROUND 2: CONTINUATION REASONING WITHOUT A RULE BANK
0086 | The second solver accepts any deterministic function on the declared 32-state
0087 | domain. It propagates possible final answers backward using observed values;
0088 | unobserved calls range over all 32 outputs. Repeated unknown calls are relaxed
0089 | independently, creating a conservative overapproximation. Singleton answers
0090 | therefore have a sound all-functions interpretation, though the approximation
0091 | can miss valid shortcuts. No 289-function bank is available to this solver.
0092 | 
0093 | The candidate updates only backward rows affected by a new observation and
0094 | stops propagation through unchanged rows. The control rebuilds every row.
0095 | Fresh confirmation: 6 seeds, 48 worlds, 384 tasks/method, including both rule-
0096 | like worlds and previously unseen exceptions/random functions. Same 24-reply cap.
