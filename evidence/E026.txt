0001 | # Experiment AD — Semantic adaptation from demonstrations
0002 | 
0003 | ## Verdict
0004 | 
0005 | **FAIL. Final-answer-only training did not bootstrap semantic induction.**
0006 | 
0007 | The attention demo encoder achieved only 8.89% macro accuracy, statistically indistinguishable from no demonstrations (8.93%) and DeepSets (8.96%). Even the shallow seen-distribution suite stayed near chance. The models did not learn routing reliably because they never acquired the prerequisite operation-inference skill.
0008 | 
0009 | Demo removal and shuffling have no effect, confirming that the demonstration channel is unused rather than merely weak. AC remains valid for fixed learned semantics; AD shows that changing semantics creates a new learning problem.

0065 | ## Decision
0066 | 
0067 | Do not scale this end-to-end objective. Introduce a developmental curriculum: first train the frozen-context adapter to predict one-step demonstration consequences or held-out demo queries, then train short programs, then deep programs, and finally remove auxiliary losses. A direct coefficient-regression oracle and an exact least-squares solver should be included to distinguish identifiability from neural optimization failure.
