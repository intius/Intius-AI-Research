0001 | WHAT THE LOCAL DYNAMICS EXPERIMENTS ESTABLISH
0002 | 2026-09-07 local date. Read with the language-model screen and final assessment.
0003 | 
0004 | Result
0005 | A balanced selective finite-difference objective improved long autonomous
0006 | rollouts in a small recurrent model when the correct nuisance geometry was
0007 | available during training. This was confirmed on five fresh data/initialization
0008 | seeds against both random-direction regularization and full backpropagation
0009 | through short rollouts. No parameters or additional inference branches were
0010 | added by the training objective.
0011 | 
0012 | This is evidence for a mechanism on a known-manifold dynamics problem. It is
0013 | not a novel principle, a general intelligence result, or evidence that a 1B
0014 | language model outperforms another model. The source audit identifies several
0015 | close conceptual predecessors. The real-language direction estimator is a
0016 | separate, harder hypothesis.
0017 | 
0018 | Confirmed experiment, 4,868 parameters in every arm
0019 | Method                        Mean 64-step MSE     Across-seed SD
0020 | RMS normalization                    0.406534             0.11115
0021 | Isotropic secant + clean             0.142391             0.03704
0022 | 4-step full BPTT                     0.227320             0.06782
0023 | 8-step full BPTT, half batch         0.147260             0.04431
0024 | Selective secant + clean             0.029152             0.00795
0025 | Exact pair normalization oracle      0.017158             0.00162
0026 | 
0027 | The selective recipe reduced mean 64-step MSE by 92.83% relative to RMS,
0028 | 79.53% relative to isotropic regularization, 87.18% relative to BPTT4, and
0029 | 80.20% relative to BPTT8. It won all five paired seeds against each control.
0030 | Mean one-step MSE was 5.19% higher than RMS, within the prewritten 25% limit.
0031 | The oracle with exact inference-time geometric projection remained better.
0032 | Numbers above describe rollout MSE, not accuracy, perplexity, or an LLM score.
0033 | 
0034 | At eight transitions, within the trained rollout range, selective repair
0035 | also improved over the RMS, random-direction, and BPTT controls. Wider
0036 | velocities and initial radial noise were reported separately in the saved
0037 | tables. These distribution shifts are modest variations of the same task.
0038 | 
0039 | What the winning objective actually does
0040 | 1. Train ordinary transition prediction on two clean example branches.
0041 | 2. Perturb a teacher state in the normal component of the student's error.
0042 | 3. Penalize the difference between the next states from the clean and
0043 |    perturbed inputs, divided by the perturbation magnitude.
0044 | 4. Keep global RMS normalization in the deployed model.
0045 | 
0046 | The two clean branches matter. A stronger or more complicated regularizer
0047 | was not automatically better. All geometric information used to select the
0048 | perturbation is training-only; the neural network alone performs the normal
0049 | arm's inference. However, that information is privileged supervision.
0050 | 
0051 | Negative results were preserved
0052 | Test 1: Ordinary normally corrupted prediction targets lost to RMS in all
0053 | five seeds. Its primary gate failed.
0054 | Test 2: A normalized normal secant term lowered long-run MSE but raised
0055 | single-step MSE by 81.57%. Its primary gate failed.
0056 | Test 3: Explicitly matching tangent changes did not fix that tradeoff. The
0057 | primary lost to a simpler normal-only control and raised one-step MSE by
0058 | 47.03%. Its primary gate failed.
0059 | Test 4: The best observed simple control from test 3 was frozen, then tested
0060 | on five new seeds with stronger full-BPTT controls. Its gate passed.
0061 | The fourth result does not convert the first three failures into successes.
0062 | 
0063 | Scope of the screen
0064 | 130 full training runs, in four sequential experiments with five fresh seeds
0065 | per experiment. Each trained model has the same 4,868-parameter residual MLP.
0066 | There were also separate excluded smoke checks. These are not 130 independent
0067 | confirmatory hypotheses or language-model runs. Hyperparameters were fixed
0068 | within each experiment; later designs were informed by earlier failures.
0069 | Five seeds provide a screening result, not a strong statistical guarantee.
0070 | 
0071 | Computation qualification
0072 | The confirmation's balanced arms use approximately 12.5 forward-equivalent
0073 | matrix-work units per update, versus approximately 12.0 for the BPTT controls.
0074 | This estimate treats backward as twice forward; it is not an exact hardware
0075 | profile. The selective arm therefore had approximately 4.2% more estimated
0076 | matrix work. All arms use the same 1,200 optimizer updates. The raw notes
0077 | report measured training times, but these tiny CPU timings do not predict
0078 | GPU throughput or establish compute efficiency for a language model.
0079 | 
0080 | The decisive weakness
0081 | Valid toy states lie on two known unit circles, so the distinction between
0082 | phase information and radial drift is handed to the trainer. An LLM does not
0083 | come with such a geometric oracle. Natural-language state errors can lie in
0084 | directions that carry rare but crucial information. Removing them can hurt
0085 | the very capabilities we want to retain. Global/PCA variance, output
0086 | sensitivity, and semantic usefulness must not be equated without evidence.
0087 | 
0088 | The task also uses two time-invariant rotations with constant velocities;
0089 | it does not include language, arbitrary factual recall, discrete branching,
0090 | or the diversity of computations expected of a general model.
