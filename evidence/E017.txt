0026 | ## Interpretation
0027 | 
0028 | A/B/C isolate unique same-distribution examples with identical update counts. D changes substrate exposure; E balances actual positive-route hops. The verdict booleans use predeclared materiality thresholds in `finalize.py`; always inspect the raw point estimates and distance table.
0029 | Performance follows physical-hop coverage far more closely than raw example count. D/E move gates closer to one, but their fixed 600-update objective stops before lossless propagation. A predeclared magnitude-matched random control with gates near 0.99 solves deep conditions, so the architecture can express the behavior; the trained failure is primarily optimization/objective coverage, not hard representational impossibility. Shuffled-source equality indicates topology-agnostic flooding rather than learned use of one graph.
0030 | 
0031 | This remains a fixed max-product/equality primitive. Training adjusts attenuation gates; it does not learn representations or discover arbitrary reasoning. Physical cells are tied in blocks of 80.
