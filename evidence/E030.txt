0005 | DECISION
0006 | OBSERVED: On the frozen synthetic duplicate-evidence task, a small selector trained on state-conditional answer-loss labels retained both required evidence groups in all 1,000 held-out episodes at a hard 2-of-8 KV-block cap. It matched a programmed exact group-count baseline and the conditional oracle.
0007 | OBSERVED: Attention, recency, and both static learned selectors retained the answer-support pair in 0/1,000 episodes. Random retention did so in 138/1,000.
0008 | INFERRED: The narrow cause is a changing marginal value: a support block is dispensable while a duplicate remains, then becomes essential after its duplicate is evicted.
0009 | REJECTED_FOR_GPU_PROMOTION: The result does not justify a shared GPU job. The exact group-count algorithm matches the candidate without training, the world relation is supplied, the cache has only eight logical blocks, and no actual LLM KVs or VRAM were measured.

0043 | ATTRIBUTION AND LIMITS
0044 | This supports one finite mechanism point: labels collected only at the full cache can miss a support token’s changing role under later evictions. Updating its predicted removal harm from the current cache state solved this synthetic redundant-evidence construction.
0045 | 
0046 | The exact group-count program also scored 1,000/1,000. It directly uses the known query-key categories, has no learned parameters, and is the simplest adequate solution in this toy. The candidate therefore demonstrates a training-target effect, not a new reasoning ability. At the eight-block scale, 4.125x more label ablations are easy to afford; the candidate feature/score work grows roughly quadratically as the cache is reduced token-by-token. This makes scaling to long contexts a separate and potentially fatal bottleneck.
0047 | 
0048 | No general language, coding, factual reasoning, transfer, or VRAM improvement is established. The external KV-retention field already includes future-utility, query-aware, confidence-aware, learned-gate, RL and online low-rank methods; the local answer-loss target is a narrow comparator, not a global novelty claim.
