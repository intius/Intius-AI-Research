0050 | What the follow-up changed
0051 | The first study beat uniform SVD but failed strict superiority over
0052 | budgeted SVD on cohortA. That outcome remains in ../RESULTS.txt.
0053 | This separate prospective follow-up spends the full708-neuron budget
0054 | and corrects later-layer inputs after earlier layers are compressed.
0055 | Learned architecture widths: [140, 19, 15, 11, 10, 16, 35, 53, 67, 83, 107, 152].
0056 | Each output regression targets the original teacher next state minus
0057 | the actual compressed state after attention. The SVD control receives
0058 | the same procedure by refitting its output factor. No runtime state
0059 | corrector, teacher or extra inference branch is retained.
0060 | 
0061 | All four model families have587,232 learned FP32 weights,2,348,928
0062 | weight bytes, and560,256 projection MACs per token. All retain the
0063 | same12-layer attention path and6,144 bytes of rebuilt RoPE buffers.
0064 | The architectural difference is nonlinear feature capacity and actual
0065 | operator/activation cost, not hidden weights or a larger compute budget.
0066 | 
0067 | Evidence and scope
0068 | The two-arm pilot selected the candidate using validation only. Six
0069 | new500-update models were trained; six original same-seed/update SVD
0070 | checkpoints were retained as additional controls. No test-selected
0071 | checkpoint was substituted. Both old and new inference models were
0072 | evaluated on the same fresh cohortB and timed in the same process.
0073 | CohortB:128 article titles absent from all WikiText2 partitions and
0074 | cohortA,513,344 scored bytes. New grammar excludes both earlier subsets.
0075 | Regularized layer-fit objectives and full-forward state consistency
0076 | passed. All12 checkpoint counts/hashes and validation scores were
0077 | independently replayed, plus four complete new articles per model.
0078 | The audit also checks all960 timing records and recomputes decisions.
0079 | 
0080 | Latency is FP32 eager CPU inference on64-byte windows, not GPU or
