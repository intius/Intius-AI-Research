0001 | RESEARCH ASSESSMENT: BETTER CAPABILITY PER STORED PARAMETER
0002 | 2026-09-07 local date. Work performed solo, locally.
0003 | 
0004 | Current conclusion
0005 | I have not demonstrated a scientific breakthrough or a 1B model that beats
0006 | current leaders. The work produced a reproducible positive result on a
0007 | controlled dynamics task, followed by a failed transfer to actual text
0008 | prediction. The tested language-model recipe is not ready for a 1B scale-up.
0009 | 
0010 | The tested idea
0011 | Reuse a model's weights across computation steps, and train the shared
0012 | transition to suppress harmless state errors while preserving information.
0013 | Use a finite-difference loss normalized by perturbation size so internal
0014 | error amplification is trained explicitly. Balance it with clean prediction
0015 | supervision. Infer candidate harmless directions from a teacher, and verify
0016 | that perturbing them barely changes the teacher's predictions.
0017 | 
0018 | Neither weight reuse, knowledge distillation, Jacobian matching, nor manifold
0019 | denoising is a new principle. The source audit identifies close prior art,
0020 | including Relaxed Recursive Transformers, Ouro, Parcae, OPRD, Flex-KD,
0021 | Jacobian matching, and manifold-based recurrent memory. No verified novelty
0022 | claim is made for the combined recipe. See 01_sources_and_novelty_audit.txt.
0023 | 
0024 | What worked in the controlled experiment
0025 | The task tracks two rotating circles. Phase carries information; radial
0026 | motion is nuisance. Exact nuisance geometry is available during training.
0027 | Every neural model has 4,868 parameters. A balanced normal-direction secant
0028 | loss plus ordinary prediction supervision passed a fresh five-seed screen.
0029 | 
0030 | Final confirmation, mean squared error after 64 autonomous steps:
0031 |   RMS normalization baseline:                 0.406534
0032 |   Random-direction regularization:            0.142391
0033 |   Four-step full backpropagation:              0.227320
0034 |   Eight-step full backpropagation, half batch:  0.147260
0035 |   Selective repair:                            0.029152
0036 |   Exact inference-time geometry oracle:        0.017158
0037 | 
0038 | Selective repair reduced rollout MSE by 80.20% relative to the eight-step
0039 | backpropagation control and won every paired seed. One-step MSE rose 5.19%,
0040 | within the prewritten 25% tolerance. Estimated matrix training work was about
0041 | 4.2% higher than the BPTT controls; this was not exact wall-time equality.
0042 | The exact geometric oracle remained better. No extra inference-time branch
0043 | or trainable weights were added by the selective loss.
0044 | 
0045 | Three earlier formulations failed their prewritten gates. Those results were
0046 | preserved. The successful configuration was frozen after exploratory work,
0047 | then confirmed on new seeds with stronger controls. There were 130 complete
0048 | toy training runs across four sequential experiments, plus excluded smoke
0049 | checks. This is one deliberately favorable dynamics problem, not 130 separate
0050 | language-model confirmations. See 21_toy_findings.txt and 19_results_tables.txt.
0051 | 
0052 | What happened on actual text
0053 | I trained a small causal byte-level Transformer teacher on Tiny Shakespeare,
0054 | then compressed it into dense and recurrent students. Teacher: 275,264 total
0055 | parameters. Students: 188,992 total parameters, a 31.3415% reduction. The
0056 | experiment included 18 student runs across three training data streams.
0057 | All students share one teacher and initial compressed weights; teacher and
0058 | initialization diversity remain untested.
0059 | 
0060 | The estimated nuisance space used the lowest 16 covariance directions in
0061 | 64-dimensional teacher states. A whole-sequence teacher-KL test accepted
0062 | about 97.56% of these perturbations, versus about 1.17% of isotropic ones.
0063 | That establishes a difference in measured teacher sensitivity at the tested
0064 | perturbation size. It does not establish universal semantic irrelevance.
0065 | 
0066 | Mean held-out test cross-entropy, nats per byte (smaller is better):
0067 |   Teacher, 275264 parameters:                        1.904111
0068 |   Dense distilled student, 188992 parameters:        1.904427
0069 |   Recurrent student, ordinary distillation:          1.908258
0070 |   Recurrent student, additional feature matching:    1.908214
0071 |   Recurrent student, verified selective repair:      1.908269
0072 | 
0073 | Selective repair produced no meaningful prediction improvement. The primary
0074 | language-screen gate failed: it required at least 5% lower mean test loss than
0075 | each recurrent control and consistent seed wins. Its mean test loss was
0076 | slightly higher than the ordinary recurrent distillation control. Repeating
0077 | the trained recurrent core four times also did not improve over its trained
0078 | two-pass configuration. The dense student slightly outperformed the looped
0079 | variants on this test split.
0080 | 
0081 | These tiny models and this narrow corpus do not rank modern 1B systems. The
0082 | near-preservation of teacher quality by ordinary compression is not our
0083 | breakthrough: it uses established methods and additional student training.
0084 | See 23_language_screen_preregistration.txt and 28_language_results_tables.txt.
0085 | 
0086 | Repair-strength diagnostic
0087 | A separate validation-only diagnostic increased the repair coefficient over
0088 | 0, 0.05, 0.5, 5, 50, starting every arm from the same saved student and using
0089 | the same 250 additional training updates. No test scores were produced by
0090 | this diagnostic, and it does not replace the failed screen.
0091 |

0093 |    0                  1.010738                        1.761515

0099 | The coefficient 5 intervention reduced measured squared sensitivity by 92.07%
0100 | but raised validation loss 1.40%. Coefficient 50 reduced sensitivity 98.61%
0101 | while raising loss 4.14%. The coefficient 0 control had the best R=2 validation
0102 | loss in this sweep. The stronger interventions changed the intended internal
0103 | metric but did not improve ordinary text prediction. This weakens the claim
0104 | that simply imposing more contraction is the missing ingredient here.
0105 | It does not rule out all methods, geometries, scales, or reasoning tasks.
0106 | See 34_strength_table_and_audit.txt.
0107 | 
0108 | What this changes scientifically
0109 | The useful lesson is that teacher insensitivity is insufficient as an
0110 | optimization target. A direction can be mostly ignored by the teacher and
0111 | still offer no useful capability gain when suppressed in the student.
0112 | Improved internal stability must be linked to an actual task bottleneck.
0113 | The current evidence supports selective repair for the toy system and
0114 | rejects the tested PCA/teacher-gated text recipe as a meaningful improvement.
0115 | 
0116 | The 1B reference architecture
0117 | I implemented an untrained causal reference with exactly 990,984,192 stored
0118 | parameters: 65,536-token tied embeddings, width 2048, SwiGLU width 5632,
0119 | grouped-query attention, 19 unique blocks, and a 15-block core that can repeat.
0120 | At four passes it executes 64 virtual blocks. Dense projection work is about
0121 | 3.05 times the one-pass configuration. Independent virtual-layer KV storage
0122 | is 0.5 GiB at batch 1/context 4096/FP16, before weights and other activations.
0123 | 
0124 | The billion-parameter shape was instantiated only on PyTorch's meta device
0125 | for parameter enumeration. No billion-weight checkpoint was allocated or
0126 | trained. Behavioral checks used a 180,800-parameter random instance and passed
0127 | causality, cached/full decoding equivalence, finite gradients, weight reuse,
0128 | and distinct cache storage per virtual layer. This is a verified software
0129 | scaffold, not a verified high-performing model.
0130 | See 12_recurrent_lm_prototype.py.txt, 13_lm_prototype_validation.txt, and
0131 | 14_one_billion_model_and_decisive_test.txt.

0146 | Decision
0147 | The evidence does not support a 1B breakthrough claim or scaling this recipe
0148 | on the basis of the toy improvement. The next credible advance must show a gain
0149 | in real language/reasoning capability under strong parameter/data/compute
0150 | controls. That remains unresolved. The workspace now contains a tested
0151 | mechanism, an explicit failed transfer, trained small models, and a concrete
0152 | 1B architecture/evaluation specification to support further research.
