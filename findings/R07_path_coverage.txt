R07 — Physical path coverage mattered more than repeated easy examples

EVIDENCE STATUS: PARTIAL SCALE-TRANSFER SUCCESS; DEEP REASONING FAILED

Distance-balanced training improved shallow transfer across graph sizes where more same-distribution data did not.

THE IDEA
For a propagation mechanism, graph size can change the distance evidence must
travel. Training examples should cover those physical distances, rather than
merely repeat the same short routes with more labels.

WHAT SUCCEEDED
Five arms began from the same neutral initialization and used 600 updates.
At 150k nominal cells, 240, 2,400 and 24,000 same-distribution unique examples
all produced the same 60.49% macro accuracy. A 2,400-example distance-balanced
arm reached 69.86%; depth-2 accuracy reached 99.31%. Macro equally weighted
five logical depths. The balanced arm still scored 50% at unseen depths 8/16.

WHY MORE ITERATIONS DID NOT FIX IT
Evidence attenuated approximately as relay_gate^hops * relation_gate^relations.
The best learned relation gate was about 0.956847; its 16th power was about
0.4937, below the fixed 0.5 decision threshold even before relay loss.
Persistent max does not accumulate repeated messages. More sweeps permit
arrival but cannot rescue a subthreshold product.

CONFOUNDS TO KEEP
Only two gates were learned; graph flooding and relation semantics were
engineered. Eighty cell lanes were tied, so 150k physical cells represented
1,875 independent units. Distance balancing also changed depth mix and
example exposures (826,400 versus 960,000), so this was not a perfectly
isolated path-distance intervention. One magnitude-matched random rule solved
deep conditions, showing a representable solution, not a working optimizer.

HOW TO BUILD ON IT
For synthetic reasoning data, log dependency distance and task complexity,
not just example count or sequence length. Match logical depth and exposure
when testing a coverage hypothesis. Applying the lesson to LoRA curricula is
a research suggestion; this study did not train an LLM on such a curriculum.

ORIGINALITY
The exact five-arm comparison and attenuation diagnosis are distinctive local
evidence. Distribution coverage as a generalization problem is prior art.
