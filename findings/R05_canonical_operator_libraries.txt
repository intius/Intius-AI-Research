R05 — Store operator shape once and recover offsets from evidence

EVIDENCE STATUS: CONFIRMED COMPACT LIBRARY RESULT; NEW-RULE TRANSFER WEAK

Canonicalization reduced a synthetic operator library while preserving or improving composition accuracy.

THE IDEA
When operators differ mainly by a modular output translation, store their
shared shape. Canonicalization subtracts f(0); an observed input/output pair
recovers the offset. This reduces duplicate library entries before inference.

WHAT SUCCEEDED
A fresh 3,072-world follow-up separated canonicalization from marginalization.
Primary accuracy and stored numeric state:
  Capped raw marginal library:    58.69%,  77,824 bytes.
  Uncapped raw marginal library:  61.72%, 129,523 bytes.
  Canonical marginal library:     63.33%,  21,280 bytes.
Against capped raw marginalization, the gain was 4.64 points with about 3.66x
less numeric state. Against uncapped raw, the point gain was 1.61 points with
6.09x less state, but adjusted p=0.0782 did not resolve accuracy superiority.

WHAT DID NOT EXPLAIN IT
Persistent hypotheses and canonical marginalization both scored 63.33%, with
40 gains and 40 losses. Persistent identity was not an established benefit.
Canonical marginal accuracy was 91.80% on ordinary new worlds and 82.62% on
long compositions, but only 18.36% on the new cubic-rule family.
The later 82.67% symbolic/library mixture mainly gained from a supplied
polynomial prior: cubic accuracy reached 100%. That is a different mechanism.

HOW TO BUILD ON IT
Deduplicate by a verified symmetry, retain the transformation needed to recover
the original function, and test unseen symmetry-breaking rules. Compare capped
and uncapped raw libraries separately so a storage limit is not mistaken for
an inference advantage. The explicit executor and mathematical priors count as
part of the system; stored numeric bytes do not equal total RAM.

ORIGINALITY
The controlled state/accuracy tradeoff is local evidence. Symmetry reduction,
canonicalization and Bayesian inference are established ideas. This is not
general semantic compression or a compressed LLM.
