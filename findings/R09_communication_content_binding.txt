R09 — Useful message content did not yield reliable source-bound recall

EVIDENCE STATUS: PARTIAL LEARNED-COMMUNICATION SUCCESS; COMPLETE RECONSTRUCTION FAILED

Messages carried causally useful symbols, while reliable reconstruction of the complete vector remained at zero.

THE IDEA
Separate three questions: does a message contain information, is that
information attached to the right source, and does the receiver use it to
produce a completely correct answer?

WHAT SUCCEEDED
Experiment I supervised reconstruction of 20 private symbols at every cell;
message/gate meanings were not separately specified. At four rounds:
  20 cells: normal symbol accuracy 25.57%; no messages 15.66%.
  64 cells: normal symbol accuracy 21.65%; no messages 13.68%.
Cross-episode shuffling reduced these to 13.80% and 12.56%. Removing one
source hurt its corresponding slot more than unrelated slots: specificity
12.367 and 9.022 percentage points. This supports useful learned communication.

WHY THAT WAS NOT ENOUGH
Complete-vector success was 0%. At 20 cells, symbol accuracy fell from 25.57%
at four rounds to 19.84% at sixteen rounds. More recurrence was not better.
Follow-up J tried widths 16-128 and a curriculum. No width reached reliable
complete-vector reconstruction. At width 32, symbol probes decoded messages
at about 99.14%, while source-slot probes were about 11.17-11.21%; complete
vectors still failed. Probe decodability is not operational competence.

HOW TO BUILD ON IT
Evaluate complete outputs alongside per-symbol scores. Use source-specific
deletion, source identity shuffling, episode shuffling and long-round curves.
For an LLM memory or agent message system, track attribution to entities and
sources as well as whether content can be recovered by an auxiliary probe.
That downstream application is a design inference, not tested in this study.

ORIGINALITY
This particular diagnostic combination is a plausible empirical contribution.
Learned communication already has direct precedents such as CommNet. Weak
source binding is a supported hypothesis here, not a fully isolated cause.
