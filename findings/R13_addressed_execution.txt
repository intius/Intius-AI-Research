R13 — Train local transitions well before judging recurrent execution

EVIDENCE STATUS: THREE-SEED SYNTHETIC EXECUTION SUCCESS; FIXED SEMANTICS

A width/training threshold turned weak register execution into strong depth and size transfer.

THE IDEA
Separate content addressing from the local state transition. A recurrent
executor can route correctly yet fail because its ordinary transition module
has not learned the supplied algebra accurately enough.

WHAT SUCCEEDED
Experiment AC compared five width/update settings over three seeds. The
113,985-parameter width-512 model at 8,000 updates reached 98.42% frozen
transfer, 99.78% exhaustive local-transition accuracy, and 97.01% on extended
depth/register tests including depth 32 and eight registers. A 75,045-parameter
model at 8,000 updates reached 96.96% transfer and 93.38% extended accuracy.
At 4,000 updates that smaller model reached only 74.67% and 60.38%.
For the primary model, erased state fell to 10.10%, shuffled source keys to
11.95%, and uniform addressing to 6.90% on frozen transfer.

FOLLOW-UP BOUNDARY
Changing operation semantics per episode defeated the learned adaptation arms.
A later hybrid used an exact semantic solver and exact differentiable modular
primitives with a 5,377-parameter learned router; it scored 100% across the
reported seen/unseen suites in three seeds. That success credits supplied
algorithms as well as routing; it is not discovery of arbitrary operations.
Maximum-consensus recovery tolerated 40% corrupted demonstrations at 32 demos
with 100% reported deep-program accuracy. Unknown-family rejection had AUROC
0.9870 and 3.52% false acceptance on its declared test.

HOW TO BUILD ON IT
Measure local transition competence and addressing independently before adding
more recurrence. Use a width-by-training-budget grid and counterfactual routing
controls. If exact tools replace learned arithmetic, explicitly report which
parts are engineered. Application to open natural-language programs is untested.

ORIGINALITY
The particular capacity diagnosis and component chain are local empirical
results. Recurrent interpreters, exact solvers and learned routing are known.
