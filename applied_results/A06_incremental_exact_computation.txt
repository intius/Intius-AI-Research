A06 — Incremental recomputation saved work without adding intelligence

EVIDENCE STATUS: EXACT COMPONENT SPEEDUPS; GENERAL CAPABILITY UNESTABLISHED

Two studies preserved exact decisions while avoiding recomputation of unaffected dependencies.

STUDY 1: CONTINUATION TABLES
A finite-state solver propagated possible final answers backward through a
program. After an observation, it updated affected rows and stopped through
unchanged rows instead of rebuilding everything.
Six fresh seeds, 48 worlds, 384 tasks/method:
  Full rebuild: 1.394092 seconds planning; 178,388 backward rows.
  Incremental:  0.887869 seconds planning;  59,458 backward rows.
All 384 trajectories and certificate-table hashes matched. Planning fell
36.312%, with reported paired world-bootstrap interval 33.724-38.785%.
Both returned 372 correct, zero wrong and 12 abstentions, making 4,043 oracle
calls. Direct execution made the SAME calls and took only 0.009021 seconds.
The incremental optimization worked; the broader query-saving gate failed.

An earlier 289-function-prior compiler reduced planning 24.624% relative to
an equivalent exact answer-aware scan, but ordinary entropy querying used
fewer probes. Outside the supplied rule family, the compiled method returned
nine wrong answers despite finite-class certificates. A certificate is only
as broad as its assumptions.

STUDY 2: REVISION PROPAGATION
In a separate synthetic acyclic knowledge graph with 20,000 propositions,
about 26,500 supplied rules and 60 scheduled edits per seed, incremental
reverse-dependency propagation matched full recomputation after every edit.
Across three seeds, median update-latency speedup was 44.75x and median
rule-check reduction 10,284.5x. Persistent layout cost 1.238x the control.
Median affected fraction was only 0.01%; the 95th percentile was 53-69%, so
tail latency approached full recomputation. Seven of 180 writes were
idempotent, a disclosed protocol deviation.
The later language writer produced no schema-valid events under its frozen
budget. Thus the graph result did not become a working language-memory result.

HOW TO USE THIS
Cache exact dependency information when edits are sparse, but measure large
revision tails, deletion/correction correctness and additional index memory.
Compare with direct execution, not only a deliberately complete rebuild.
Abstract interpretation, truth maintenance and incremental views are known;
these are bounded implementation measurements.
