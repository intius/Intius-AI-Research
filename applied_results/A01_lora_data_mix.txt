A01 — A checked four-part data mix improved a small LoRA

EVIDENCE STATUS: SMALL FROZEN CONFIRMATION PASSED; ESTABLISHED METHOD

Continuing a rank-8 adapter improved a separate public subset from 27/48 to 35/48, with a generation-time cost.

WHY THIS IS AN APPLIED APPENDIX
LoRA, supervised distillation and replay are known. The reusable information
is the exact data mixture, the held-out observation and the failed successors.

RECIPE THAT WAS ACTUALLY TESTED
Base: Nanbeige/Nanbeige4.2-3B, revision
b82e54bd609793562a75cbf9337970a93369eab5. Continue the existing Stage 1 rank-8
LoRA for 256 updates, accumulation 4, AdamW learning rate 4e-5, FP16 base,
gradient checkpointing, with maximum 512 prompt-plus-completion tokens.
The 1,024-row mix contained four equal groups:
  256 OpenMathInstruct-2 integer-answer examples with checked final numbers;
  256 Tulu 3 persona instruction-following examples;
  256 Tulu 3 persona code examples containing parseable Python functions;
  256 verified synthetic replay examples from the original training pool.
Exact evaluation-prompt overlap was excluded. A matching boxed number is not
a proof of every reasoning step; parseable code is not necessarily correct.
The setup includes all earlier Stage 1 training and the pretrained backbone.

BEST EVIDENCE
A separate frozen 48-question suite compared unchanged Stage 1 and Stage 2:
  MMLU-Pro subset: 11/24 -> 13/24.
  GSM8K subset:    16/24 -> 22/24.
  Total:          27/48 -> 35/48.
Ten cases favored Stage 2 and two favored Stage 1. Exact paired p=0.038574;
the reported paired 95% bootstrap interval was +4.17 to +31.25 points.
Both used the same T4, prompt construction, seed base 73091, 512-token cap,
temperature 0.6, top-p 0.95 and top-k 20. The set was disjoint from the earlier
selection subset, but possible pretraining overlap remains unknown. One
training trajectory and one sampled generation per item are limited evidence.

COST AND RETENTION
Stage 2 used 7,087 output tokens and 1,034.55 seconds, versus 3,304 tokens and
511.52 seconds for Stage 1. Stage 2 fitting took 1,015.92 seconds and peaked
at 9,791,131,136 allocated GPU bytes. The selected system stored 4,181,785,600
total parameters, including embeddings and 11,984,896 adapter parameters;
the conventional 3B label refers to non-embedding scale.
Earlier retention checks tied generated holdout at 15/24 and direct tasks at
24/24, while IFEval fell 21/24 to 20/24 and dialogue 12/12 to 11/12.

FOLLOW-UPS
Stage 3's larger 7,168-row mix gained only 2/48 public cases versus Stage 2,
below its +4 gate; Stage 2 remained selected. Key aliasing saved about 25%
of KV storage on narrow retrieval, but later direct accuracy fell 24/24 to
18/24. Persistent-neuron and attention-kernel variants did not beat matched
controls enough to promote. These do not strengthen the LoRA claim.

HOW TO USE THIS
Treat the four-way mixture and retention gates as a reproducible starting
point for an ablation, not an optimized universal ratio. The study did not
isolate which data group caused the gain. Keep independent confirmation and
report output length. The third-party datasets and weights are not included
in this text release; source revisions appear in the evidence snapshot.
