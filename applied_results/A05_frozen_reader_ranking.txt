A05 — Improve answer selection without updating the reader

EVIDENCE STATUS: CROSS-DATASET COMPONENT SUCCESS; ESTABLISHED RETRIEVAL METHODS

Small rankers improved a frozen extractive reader, with a smaller gain after transfer to another dataset.

THE RECIPE
Character TF-IDF retrieves candidate paragraphs. A GloVe/character/BiDAF-style
reader proposes answer spans. A separate ranker learns which frozen candidate
answer to choose from retrieval, span and relative-score features. The
ranker's target is candidate-answer F1 on training questions.

WHAT SUCCEEDED
Experiment BC reused 1,000 SQuAD validation questions and 2,067 validation
contexts. The BB reader's raw top-five token F1 was 53.64%; pairwise XGBoost
raised it to 56.91%, with gains of 3.24, 2.58 and 4.01 points in the three
reader branches. A 3,201-parameter listwise MLP independently reached 56.94%.
All reader checkpoint hashes and original predictions remained unchanged.

BD transferred the frozen readers, rankers and normalization to a deterministic
answer-bearing HotpotQA distractor subset without fitting HotpotQA labels.
Raw top-five F1 was 25.63%; pairwise selection reached 27.22% (about +1.60
points before rounding). Gold-context F1 was 51.76%; top-five answer oracle
46.27%. This was subset extractive QA, not the full HotpotQA benchmark or a
demonstration of multi-hop reasoning.

RESOURCES AND LIMITS
The BB reader had 6,727,139 learned parameters and also used pretrained static
GloVe knowledge. BC feature extraction peaked at 207,316,992 allocated CUDA
bytes and 3,036,246,016 host RSS bytes; ranking ran on CPU. A small ranker does
not eliminate reader, retrieval-index or corpus costs. Repeated SQuAD
development use and shared reader lineage limit independent confirmation.
Later multidomain and richer-ranker screens did not deliver their required
additional gains.

HOW TO USE THIS
Keep a competent reader frozen while learning calibrated selection. Include
retrieval-only, raw-span and answer-oracle controls, then transfer unchanged
to another dataset. This can guide retrieval systems around an LLM, but this
archive did not prove the same gains for LLM-generated answers. BiDAF,
GloVe, TF-IDF and ranking are established methods.
