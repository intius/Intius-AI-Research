KEEP THESE QUALIFICATIONS ATTACHED TO THE POSITIVE RESULTS

R01: 5.891x is an isolated exact-likelihood component. The weak-prior text
comparison saved 45.589% training time; the stronger-prior comparison saved
only 6.193% and failed its combined gate. These are not interchangeable.

R02: 99.634% applies to six constrained quadratic Boolean worlds. Cubic
encodings and multi-effect actions caused complete abstention. Model JSON
bytes are not total RAM. The text renderer was handwritten.

R03: Stage 1 scores near 0.9985 are regression scores, not percent-correct.
Stage 2 near-perfect swaps used the same learned reference standard. Layout
changes and incompatible protocol versions failed; long chains accumulated
errors. A later input-order repair is a known architectural/data fix.

R04/R05: Cross terms gave a matched +6.07-point neural gain. The much larger
symbolic-mixture gain came mainly from a supplied polynomial prior. These
are different results. Neither establishes quantum advantage.

R06: Selective repair improved the known-geometry toy but not actual text.
Reducing measured sensitivity was insufficient to improve language loss.

R07: Distance coverage improved shallow transfer; deeper unseen reasoning
stayed at chance. Depth mix and total example exposure were also changed.

R08: Population scaling did not show a communication benefit. The 1,959
shared values exclude a 3.725M-parameter upstream backbone and growing state.

R09: Useful message content coexisted with zero complete-vector success.
R10: Adaptive opening beat random opening but lost to exact algebraic closure
and did not beat diagonal operators on ordinary held-out edges.
R11: A simpler zero-preserving linear control matched null subtraction.
R12: Shared normalized keys already matched at initialization; only the
independent-key arm demonstrated learning that matching behavior.
R13: Fixed-operation execution was strong; new semantics required engineered
solvers/primitives in the successful follow-up.
R14: Retaining two of eight synthetic blocks is not 75% measured VRAM savings.

A01: The first public subset helped select Stage 2. The separate 48-question
confirmation is better evidence, but small and possibly pretraining-exposed.
It also used about twice the output tokens and time. No peer-model superiority
or universal recipe follows. Stage 3 and kernel/cache follow-ups did not promote.
A02: Faster complete short-job execution had slower warm steps. Accuracy
superiority over dense was statistically unresolved.
A03: The corrected time-to-quality confirmation had one seed; earlier failed
rules were not relabeled as passing.
A04: Strict all-controls pruning superiority failed. A tiny byte-model win
did not establish retention of a stronger pretrained model's capabilities.
A05: These are selected extractive-QA datasets, not general dialogue or full
HotpotQA multi-hop results. Pretrained GloVe knowledge still counts.
A06: Exact recomputation savings did not beat direct execution on oracle
efficiency; language-grounded revision memory did not pass its writer gate.

OTHER EARLY HEADLINES NOT PROMOTED
Experiment C reachability was already present at initialization: trained and
initial predictions agreed on all 11,200 normal decisions. It is not evidence
that training learned the advertised reachability capability.
U's short-budget GRU/cell hybrid gain did not persist in V's longer matched
training. The newer ordinary GRU controls won the relevant final losses.
The 27B-under-8-GiB observation was synthetic placement with slow decode,
not a matched-quality success. D54 strict zeros were dominated by malformed
answer schemas, not clean measurements of underlying reasoning ability.
SMF numerical-gradient checks passed, but learning/fission were never reached.
Consciousness/self-binding work did not demonstrate phenomenality or AGI.

These corrections explain selection; they are not a catalog of every failed
experiment, nor proofs that the broader research families cannot succeed.
