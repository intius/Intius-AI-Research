Intius+AI: SELECTED RESEARCH FINDINGS

This collection extracts useful measured successes from a much larger research
archive. It is meant to help people design experiments, build model components,
and choose better training and evaluation procedures.

Start with 01_READING_MAP.txt. Each finding explains the mechanism, actual
measurement, relevant control, failure boundary and possible application.

THE NOVELTY STANDARD
- No world-first invention is certified by this release. The strongest material
is specific experimental evidence, diagnostic designs and implementation
details whose broader originality remains unresolved. Public popularity was
not measured. Established techniques are named and credited, not rebranded.
The applied appendix is optional for readers interested only in distinctive
experiments; it includes the directly useful LoRA recipe.

THE SUCCESS STANDARD
- A positive component can survive even when a broader experiment fails. Every
card keeps that distinction explicit. A toy-world success is not a general
language-model result. A component speedup is not a full-training speedup.

HOW STRONG IS THE EVIDENCE?
- The cards summarize local reports and their recorded audits. This curation
checked sources, selected numerical records, arithmetic and packaging. It did
not retrain models or independently reproduce all experiments. Most results
are small, synthetic or development-set observations. The archive contains
no demonstrated AGI, frontier parity or universal efficiency breakthrough.

This is a self-contained research-text collection, not a runnable reproduction
repository, model-weight release or task-training dataset. Original scripts,
checkpoints and raw task corpora remain in the source archive. No publication
or upload was performed while preparing this folder.
